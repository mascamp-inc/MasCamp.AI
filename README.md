## About MasCamp.AI™

MasCamp.AI™ is an artificial intelligence initiative developed by **MasCamp Inc.**, a Canadian technology company building digital solutions for the Carnival industry.

MasCamp.AI represents the next stage in the evolution of the MasCamp™ ecosystem, introducing intelligent technologies designed to improve access to information, support business operations, and connect people with relevant knowledge, resources, and services.

The initiative extends beyond conventional AI-powered conversations. Its long-term objective is to establish an interconnected intelligence platform that supports individuals, businesses, organizations, and digital applications throughout the Carnival ecosystem.

This public repository documents the history, significant milestones, and ongoing evolution of MasCamp.AI™.

---

## Our Vision

To establish MasCamp.AI™ as a trusted intelligence engine connecting Carnival culture, commerce, education, businesses, and technology.

By making knowledge more accessible and digital experiences more intelligent, MasCamp.AI aims to support the continued growth and modernization of the global Carnival ecosystem.

---

## Our Mission

To connect people, information, and resources through artificial intelligence while improving organizational efficiency, expanding access to Carnival knowledge, and creating new opportunities for businesses and communities.

MasCamp.AI is guided by three primary objectives:

### Organizational Intelligence

Support businesses and organizations with intelligent access to information, improved decision-making, and more efficient operations.

### Carnival Knowledge & Discovery

Help individuals explore Carnival culture, traditions, costume design, craftsmanship, events, education, and related services.

Connect users with relevant businesses, creators, instructors, suppliers, and resources throughout the Carnival community.

### Connected Ecosystem Intelligence

Enable intelligent experiences across MasCamp™ platforms and services, creating a more connected digital environment for Carnival participants, businesses, and organizations.
