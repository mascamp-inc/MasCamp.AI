## About MasCamp.AI™

MasCamp.AI™ is an artificial intelligence initiative developed by MasCamp Inc., a Canadian technology company focused on digital solutions for the Carnival industry.

The initiative represents the continued evolution of the MasCamp™ ecosystem, exploring how artificial intelligence can improve access to knowledge, information, and digital resources.

This public repository documents the history, development milestones, and evolution of MasCamp.AI.

> **Repository Purpose:** Public historical documentation, significant milestones, and official announcements. Active software development is maintained separately.
