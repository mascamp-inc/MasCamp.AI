## About MasCamp.AI™

MasCamp.AI™ is an artificial intelligence initiative developed by **MasCamp Inc.**, a Canadian technology company building digital solutions for the Carnival industry.

MasCamp.AI represents the next stage in the evolution of the MasCamp™ ecosystem, introducing intelligent technologies designed to improve access to information, support business operations, and connect people with relevant knowledge, resources, and services.

The initiative extends beyond conventional AI-powered conversations. Its long-term objective is to establish an interconnected intelligence platform that supports individuals, businesses, organizations, and digital applications throughout the Carnival ecosystem.

This public repository documents the history, significant milestones, and ongoing evolution of MasCamp.AI™.

---

## Our Vision

To establish MasCamp.AI™ as a trusted intelligence engine connecting Carnival culture, commerce, education, businesses, and technology.

By making knowledge more accessible and digital experiences more intelligent, MasCamp.AI aims to support the continued growth and modernization of the global Carnival ecosystem.

---

## Our Mission

To connect people, information, and resources through artificial intelligence while improving organizational efficiency, expanding access to Carnival knowledge, and creating new opportunities for businesses and communities.

MasCamp.AI is guided by three primary objectives:

### Organizational Intelligence

Support businesses and organizations with intelligent access to information, improved decision-making, and more efficient operations.

### Carnival Knowledge & Discovery

Help individuals explore Carnival culture, traditions, costume design, craftsmanship, events, education, and related services.

Connect users with relevant businesses, creators, instructors, suppliers, and resources throughout the Carnival community.

### Connected Ecosystem Intelligence

Enable intelligent experiences across MasCamp™ platforms and services, creating a more connected digital environment for Carnival participants, businesses, and organizations.

## Development History

### 2026 — The Beginning

**October 2026 — MasCamp.AI Introduced**

- Established MasCamp.AI™ as an initiative of MasCamp Inc.
- Introduced the vision of an intelligence engine supporting the MasCamp™ ecosystem.
- Began development of the initiative's foundational capabilities.
- Defined the initial direction for organizational intelligence and public Carnival knowledge.

**October 23, 2026 — Public Website Preview**

- Introduced the MasCamp.AI public website at [mascamp.ai](https://mascamp.ai/).
- Established the initiative's initial public presence.

**October 24, 2026 — Public Development Record**

- Established the official public GitHub repository.
- Began documenting the initiative's history and development milestones.
- Created a dedicated public record separate from active software development.

**October 2026 — Initial Intelligence Development**

- Established the initial foundation for MasCamp.AI's organizational intelligence capabilities.
- Began evaluating how artificial intelligence could improve access to organizational knowledge.
- Identified opportunities to improve information discovery, contextual understanding, and the quality of AI-generated responses.

**November 13, 2026: Public Carnival Q&A launched**

- Launched public Carnival question-and-answer at [mascamp.ai](https://mascamp.ai/): mas bands, costumes, registration, events, music, suppliers, workshops and Carnival business.
- Carnival-only by design: unrelated questions are politely redirected, and follow-up questions keep their context.
- Answers draw first on knowledge reviewed by the MasCamp team, and every answer is saved and reused.
- Introduced a free daily question for every visitor, with feedback on every answer.

**November 13–15, 2026: Team Intelligence Console**

- Introduced a private console for the MasCamp team to review questions, answers and feedback.
- Added a curated Carnival knowledge base, a Carnival dictionary, and profiles of people, mas bands, events, places and organizations, connected through links, tags and categories.
- Added versioned rules and content, so every change is recorded and can be restored.
- Established a human-review principle: AI-written facts are never published as verified until the team approves them.

**November 14, 2026: Learning From Every Question**

- Questions are automatically sorted into Carnival categories and grouped by intent.
- AI-assisted suggestions for new keywords, topics, words and profiles, each reviewed by a person before use.
- Public web research for Carnival names not yet in the knowledge base, saved with sources and reused.
- Knowledge gaps are recorded so the team can add what visitors are looking for.

**November 15, 2026: Analytics & Directory Opportunities**

- Introduced analytics on Carnival demand: trends, busiest times, categories, most-asked and not-yet-answered questions, and feedback.
- Introduced directory opportunities: which suppliers, workshops, mas bands, designers and services people ask for, by city, and where no listing exists yet.
- Improved directory matching so listings appear only when they match what a visitor actually asked, in the city they named.

**November 15, 2026: Neutral & Independent Principle**

- Established MasCamp.AI as a neutral, independent source of Carnival knowledge: fair to every band, event and Carnival, with no product promotion and no access to orders, payments or customer accounts.
- Published an internal training guide for the MasCamp team covering knowledge review, Carnival fundamentals and the neutral-and-independent principle.
